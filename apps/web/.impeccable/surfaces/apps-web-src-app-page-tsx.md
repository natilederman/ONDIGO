## Scope

ONDIGO marketing landing page (`apps/web/src/app/page.tsx`). Visitor mode: Persuade.
Primary audience: senders. Secondary path: drivers. Demo/portfolio build, so illustrative
content is allowed and labelled; commercial claims are not.

## Direction contract

THESIS: ONDIGO is a timetable of departures that happen to have room in them. The page is
that table. It refuses the category default arrangement: a map with a route line in the hero,
three equal feature cards, a trust-badge row.

OWN-WORLD: Swiss railway timetable and station typography. One grotesk (Archivo) at few sizes,
ranked by weight and tracking. Hairline rules at 1px carry all structure; no cards, no shadows,
no rounded containers except the pill primary action. Tabular lining numerals throughout.
Ground is paper white with graphite gradient fields as the only tonal movement. Palette is
ink #0A0B0D, paper #FFFFFF, ash #F4F4F5, rule #E3E4E6, steel #8A8F98, and one signal #E5342A.

STORY: The visitor understands that someone is already driving their route today; believes the
handoff is structurally safe because escrow, photo verification and ratings are shown working
rather than asserted; and posts a request.

FIRST VIEWPORT: No centered hero. A slim single-line header, then a departure table filling
the viewport. Its header row carries the section label on the left and the primary action on
the right. The value proposition sits as the table's caption beneath that row, not stacked
above it. Rows show route, item, vehicle, price and a live countdown; the route and countdown
numerals are the largest ink on the page, larger than the headline. Exactly one row stands in
signal red because its auction closes soonest.

FORM: Swiss railway timetable, candidate 3 of the grounded list, assigned by the roll.
Seed key ef96947c.

RAISES: Line-form state, not hue (from Emission-line rail, declined). One accent with exactly
one meaning (from VU meter bridge, declined). Type as matter at monumental scale (from
Alphabet storm, declined).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Constraints

Payments are simulated and the panic button is not real dispatch; both stay labelled.
Testimonials in the incumbent code are invented demo content and must not be escalated into
metrics or named customers.

## Unresolved

Whether this direction rolls out to the full site, and to the Expo app, pending user review of
this one page.
